Password Policy

To safeguard your identity, passwords must satisfy all the complexity rules specified below:

Minimum 8 characters
At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
At least one number (0-9)
At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
Cryptographic Hashing

Your password is never stored as plain text. It is automatically converted into an irreversible salted hash using Bcrypt before database storage.
